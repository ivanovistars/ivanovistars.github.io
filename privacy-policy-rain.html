Privacy Policy

App: Rain Sounds: Sleep Offline

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Rain Sounds: Sleep Offline does not directly collect, harvest, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All audio preferences, custom mixes, and timer configurations remain strictly local on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), diagnostic telemetry, and interaction metrics to serve relevant ads. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for full details.

3. Offline Architecture

Sound generation and sleep mixer features are designed to work offline without requiring constant internet access.
