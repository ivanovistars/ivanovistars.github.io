Privacy Policy — Block Maker for Minecraft

Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Summary: Block Maker for Minecraft does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data, account registrations, photos, or location information. All 3D blocks, weapons, foods, and armor sets you create are generated and saved locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, request, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from users of Block Maker for Minecraft. The application requires no user account, login, email address, phone number, or personal profile.

2. Local File Creation & Storage

All pixel art textures and .mcaddon add-on packages created within the app are processed locally on your device. When you tap "Export to Minecraft (.mcaddon)", the generated file is saved to your device's local storage/Downloads folder so you can import it into Minecraft Bedrock Edition. These files are never uploaded to external servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android application uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements when exporting add-on files. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personal device identifiers (such as your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based country/region, and basic diagnostic/ad-performance data) to serve and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. All creative tools operate offline on the user's device, and no user chat, social sharing, or personal data input exists in the app.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store store listing page for Block Maker for Minecraft.
