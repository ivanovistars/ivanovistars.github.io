Privacy Policy

Symbol Lore: Lava Burn does not directly collect, harvest, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or location). All game progress, counters, and settings remain strictly local on your device.

The Android application integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements at the top of the screen. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), diagnostic telemetry, and interaction metrics to serve relevant ads. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for full details.

3. Offline Audio & Gameplay

All symbol, fruit, and vegetable sorting mechanics and Text-to-Speech (TTS) voice pronunciations operate locally using your device's built-in speech engine without requiring user accounts or cloud storage.
